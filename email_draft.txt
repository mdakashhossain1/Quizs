1. GOOGLE LOGIN FIX (SHA KEY UPDATE)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
• Why it happened: When an app is published, Google Play automatically re-signs it with a new Production App Signing Key. Because this production SHA key is generated after launch, it had to be registered with Firebase.
• The Fix: We extracted the production SHA-1 key (2D:68:DB:...) from Play Console and linked it with your Firebase & Google Cloud accounts. Google Login is now 100% active and working.
